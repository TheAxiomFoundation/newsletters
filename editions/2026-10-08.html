Benchmark snapshot
PolicyBench: the gap closed fast
PolicyBench is an open benchmark from the Axiom Foundation and PolicyEngine, scoring 46 models on United States household tax and benefit calculations. A month ago the best model missed about one household in nine; on the October 5 board it misses about one in twenty, and nine of the top ten now clear 90 percent. See the full board →

Funding news

HOLD THE SEND until PBIF announces. They go first and review our copy, and the LoA bars naming an amount. Copy below is the cleared version — selected, not funded, no state named

The Axiom Foundation has been selected for the Public Benefit Innovation Fund’s Spring 2026 cohort. We will work with a state agency to test whether open, executable encodings of SNAP eligibility rules can reproduce that agency’s own determinations, with every result traced back to the provision of law behind it. We have committed to publishing what we learn under an open license, including the parts that do not work.
