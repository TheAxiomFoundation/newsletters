Feature

New app interface

The Axiom App has a new interface. It opens on a map shaded by how much of each jurisdiction we have encoded, so you can see where the corpus runs deep and where it is still thin before you search for anything. Pick a rule and the canvas follows the selection, the browser’s back button walks the same steps the app’s own Back does, and node details pair short facts rather than stacking every one.

Pavel is writing up what changed and why, and we will link his post here. PM: post and URL — the feature leads on it, so this is the blocker for the send

Benchmark snapshot
PolicyBench: the gap closed fast
PolicyBench is an open benchmark from the Axiom Foundation and PolicyEngine, scoring 46 models on United States household tax and benefit calculations. A month ago the best model missed about one household in nine; on the October 5 board it misses about one in twenty, and nine of the top ten now clear 90 percent. See the full board →

product
Product updates

•The corpus went broad rather than deep. 225 jurisdictions, 35,252 source documents and 357,478 provisions on 5 October, against 200, 9,913 and 129,356 a month ago, while encodings moved only from 4,795 to 4,803. PM: confirm what drove the jump
•The chatbot preview is open at axiom.org/chatbot. Ask about a benefit or a tax and the dollar amounts come from the Axiom rules engine rather than the model’s memory, with the tool calls under each answer showing the calculation. It covers 34 programs, answers are estimates rather than determinations, and the page says so before you type anything.
•Receipt reached 0.6.3 and the hosted API now runs each parity case against its external source daily, serving the latest result.
