Feature

Traceable, transparent rules for public benefits

The Axiom Foundation is one of eleven projects in the Public Benefit Innovation Fund’s Spring 2026 cohort, announced on October 22. We will work with a state agency to test open encodings of SNAP (food assistance) eligibility rules — rules written as software that people can read and computers can run, with every provision linked to the law it comes from.

The transparently encoded rules become a foundation states can build on: government systems give families faster, more accurate decisions, and the same rules can ground the AI tools states are adopting. We will publish the encodings and what we learn under an open license, including the parts that do not work.

Benchmark snapshot
GPT-6 Sol takes the lead
PolicyBench is an open benchmark from the Axiom Foundation and PolicyEngine, scoring 50 models on United States household tax and benefit calculations. GPT-6 Sol leads at 95.2 percent of sampled households answered to within a dollar, and every model in the top ten now clears 90 percent, where a month ago only the leader cleared 88. The board also turned on itself this autumn: we audited the answer key against the law, using the models’ misses to find bugs in our own reference engine. See the full board →
