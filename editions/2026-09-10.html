product
Product updates

The corpus · 28 August 2026
Re-pull axiom.org/coverage on Sep 9 — update all three figures and this date
9,069 source documents · 94,366 provisions · 4,155 RuleSpec encodings
Every figure is live on the coverage page — check them against this email whenever you like.
•Three countries joined the corpus. Canada, with the Canada Workers Benefit as administered by the CRA; Belgium, with a worker personal-income-tax pipeline; and Denmark, which meant teaching the pipeline to read Retsinformation’s LexDania format first.
•The corpus grew by about a third. Just over 3,000 RuleSpec encodings at launch on 28 July, 4,155 on 28 August.
•US coverage runs deepest where we have focused. Colorado leads at 1,010 encodings, with North Carolina at 489 and California at 389. Breadth of source documents came first, and depth follows program by program.
•Verification got a cutover process. Four measured gates a package has to clear, rather than a judgment call someone makes, plus guards that catch a package quietly dropping coverage. Being open is not the differentiating part; being checkable is.
•The API can answer questions about time. You can now ask what a rule computed on a date in the past, not only what it computes today — the whole job for anyone reconstructing a determination.
